The Assure Story

Millions of Indians trust chit funds — yet opacity, fraud, and manual processes erode that trust daily. We set out to fix it.

Assure Chit Funds was founded by fintech veterans who saw the gap between how chit funds should work and how they actually did. We built a platform where every auction is recorded on a transparent ledger, every payment is tracked in real-time, and every member can verify their chit group activity from their phone.

Today we operate 50+ active chit groups, serve 12,500+ members across Telangana & Andhra Pradesh, and have disbursed ₹45 Crore+ in chit value — with zero defaults.

256-bit SSL, biometric authentication, SBI payment rails, and fraud-detection AI that monitors every transaction in real time.

100% Transparent Auctions
