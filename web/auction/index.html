Auction Process

How Our Auctions Work

Simple, fair, transparent — just like it should be.

1
Auction Opens

On a fixed date each month, the auction opens for 24 hours. All enrolled members receive an app notification & email alert.

→
2
Members Bid

Members submit their bid — the amount they're willing to discount. Lower bids win, but smart bidders maximize dividends for non-winners.

→
3
Winner Gets Paid

Lowest bidder wins the prize pot — credited to their bank same day. Dividend is distributed equally to all other members by evening.
