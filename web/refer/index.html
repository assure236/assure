Referral FAQ

Common Referral Questions

When do I receive my referral bonus?▲
Your ₹500 bonus is credited automatically within 24 hours of your referred friend making their first monthly instalment payment. You'll receive an SMS and app notification confirming the credit.
Is there a limit on the number of referrals?▲
No limit at all! You can refer as many people as you like. If you refer 100 friends, you earn ₹50,000. The referral program has no cap — the more you share, the more you earn.
What if my friend registers but doesn't join a group?▲
The ₹500 bonus is triggered when your friend makes their first instalment payment (i.e., actively joins a chit group). Registration alone does not trigger the bonus. Encourage your friend to join quickly!
Can I refer existing Assure members?▲
No — the referral bonus applies only when the person you refer is a new member who hasn't previously registered with Assure. Duplicate or self-referrals are not counted.
