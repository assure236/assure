FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you join Assure Chit Funds.

What is a chit fund and how does it work?
A chit fund is a rotating savings & credit scheme. A group of members pool a fixed amount monthly (e.g. 20 members x ₹5,000 = ₹1 lakh/month). Each month, an auction decides who gets the prize pot. The person who accepts the lowest amount wins the pot. The discount they accept is divided among other members as dividend. The process repeats till every member has received once.
Is Assure Chit Funds legal and government registered?
Yes, absolutely. We are registered under the Telangana Chit Funds Act, 1971 and comply with the Chit Funds Act, 1982 (Central). We are DPIIT Startup India recognised and RBI compliant. Our registration details and annual audit reports are publicly available on our website.
How much can I earn as dividend per month?
Dividend depends on the auction discount. Historically, winning bids in our groups average a 25-35% discount. For a ₹5 lakh chit with 24 members, a 30% discount (₹1.5L) divided by 24 = ₹6,250 dividend for each member in that month. Use our Dividend Calculator above to estimate your earnings.
Can I join if I don't have a high CIBIL score?
Yes! While we prefer 650+ CIBIL, it is not mandatory for all schemes. Our Starter and Silver chits have relaxed eligibility. Regular participation in our chit fund also helps improve your CIBIL score over time by demonstrating consistent repayment behaviour.
What happens if I miss a monthly instalment?
You have a 5-day grace period after the due date. After that, a nominal late fee applies (as per your chit agreement). If you miss 3 consecutive months, your membership may be suspended. We recommend enabling Auto-Pay in the app to never miss a payment.
How do I receive my prize money or dividend?
Prize money (if you win the auction) is transferred to your registered bank account within the same business day via NEFT/IMPS. Monthly dividends are auto-credited to all member accounts within 3 business days of the auction date.
